Subject: Degree Grade Point Average (DGPA) for Ph.D. programme continuation for
the students enrolled from July 2019 onwards
The Senate in its 30th Meeting held on 2nd September 2022 approved implementation of Degree
Grade Point Average (DGPA) for continuation of Ph.D. programme for the students enrolled from July
2019 onwards. DGPA shall be defined with respect to the best performance of the student in minimum
number of credits. Further, the CGPA of 7.5 or DGPA shall be compulsory toward completion of
courseworkforaPhD student.
