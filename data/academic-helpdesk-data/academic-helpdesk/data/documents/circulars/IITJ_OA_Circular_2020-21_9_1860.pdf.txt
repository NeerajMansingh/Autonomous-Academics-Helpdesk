Subject: Comprehensive, SOTA & Qualifier requirements for M. Tech.-Ph.D. and Part Time Ph.D.
students
In addition to the regulations (For Students enrolled from July 2019 onwards) clause 26.5 the
Comprehensive, SOTA and Qualifier requirements for M. Tech.-Ph.D. and Part Time Ph.D. students is as
under:
1.
The 16 credits associated with the M. Tech Projects are to be converted to the Research Proposal
credits for the Dual Degree students. They need not register for additional Research Proposal
courses. They still need to appear for a Research Proposal presentation that should include a
plan of PhD work
2.
The Comprehensive Examination must be cleared before SOTA.
3.
 The Dual Degree students need to complete comprehensive examination and SOTA within 4
semesters of their registration (31st July for July admission and 31st December for December
admission).
4.
 The Qualifier requirements must be completed before the beginning of the 5th semester for Dual
Degree students. Only in special cases subject to approval the same can be completed by 5th
Semester.
5.
students. Mid Term evaluation in the mid of second trimester on 18th Jan 2021 will be considered
as the 1st presentation.
6.
The Final presentation (M. Tech project part 2) will be the research proposal defense for dual
degree students which will be evaluated by SRC. This can be completed only after the successful
completion of comprehensive examination and SOTA.
7.
For Dual Degree students no separate committee other than SRC is to be formed for evaluation.
8.
A Continuation grade will be awarded until the successful defense of the research proposal.
9.
The Dual degree students need to submit a report of maximum 10 pages approved by the SRC
Committee to the office of academics.
10. For Part Time PhD students, the comprehensive examination must be completed within 4
semesters (31st July for July admission and 31st December for December admission) and SOTA
within 5 semesters (31st December for July admission and 31st July for December admission).
11. Comprehensive dates for A.Y. 2021 from January to July is April 26 to 30 April 2021.
This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
