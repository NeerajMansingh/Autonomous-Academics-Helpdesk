Annexure-I
Guidelines for the TA Allotment
In the last few years, the Institute has seen enormous growth in terms.of number of faculty
and students. In order to optimize the resources, teaching-learning process and research
ecosystem at the institute, it is proposed to adopt the following guidelines TA allotment,
1. TAs would be primarily allocated to courses and not to the faculty members. The
allocation would be carried out by the DRC;
2. A student will be assigned to a course he/she is better equipped to be a TA for
(equipped is defined by the concerned faculty in terms of what knowledge/skills
he/ she expects the student to possess);
3. The TA duties must be evenly distributed among all eligible students. For example,
students can be assigned UG Laboratory, PG Laboratory, and Theory courses on
rotation. The Instructors in lecture only courses. must ensure that the assigned TAs
are discussing solutions of assignments and quizzes with students;
4. The TAs must discuss, plan and prepare for different aspects of the course;
5. Students should be utilized as a TA for 8 hours per week. The DRC must ensure
that TAs are used efficiently and optimally. No student should be assigned TA duty
for more than 8 hours a week;
6. Course Priority for TA allocation
. B.Tech. compulsory courses
would be givenpriority
over
M.Tech./M.Sc./Ph.D. compulsory courses
. Courses with more students would be given priority over courses with a
small number of students
. Compulsory courses would be given priority over elective courses..
. Courses with laboratory and/or tutorial will be given priority over lecture
only courses
. Courses with a laboratory will be given priority over courses with a tutorial;
7. Lecture only courses would be allocated 1 TA per 60 students;
8. Courses with tutorial will be allocated 1 TA per 40 students;
9. Course with a laboratory will be allocated 1 TA per 20 students;
10. Lab only'courses will also be allotted 1 TA per 20 students;
11. Laboratory/Tutorial Courses with students less than five and Theory only courses
with students less than 20 will not be considered for TA allocation;
12. The following courses will also not be considered for allotment of TA in the first
round of allocation
a. Theory only courses with students less than 30
b. Course with tutorial with students less than 10
c. Course with laboratory/Lab only courses with studenits less than 10

13. The DRC Convenor will share the list of surplus TAs, after the first round of TA
duty allocation based on the above guidelines, to the Office of Academics. The Office
of Academics will allocate TA duties for institute-wide Non-graded courses and
Institute-wide common courses with shortage of TAs, if required;
14. After allocating TAs for Non-graded and Institute-wide common courses with the
shortage of TAs, the surplus TAs would be reassigned to the respective departments.
The department can perform the second round of TA duty allocation for the courses
listedinitem12above;
15. The first round of TA allotment must happen at least 2 weeks before the start of
the semester. The TAs must use this time to interact with the instructor and plan for
thesemester;
16. The final allotment of TAs must be completed by all departments by the late date
ofaddanddropofcourses;
17. IDRP students will be also allotted TA duties for NG courses;
18. TA in a PG course should not have registered as a student in the same course;
19. As far as possible, students in their final year of the Ph.D. program should not be
assigned TA duty in a laboratory; and
20. For an optimal conduct of courses with the help TAs, the academic units are
requested to adhere to the time-lines proposed in the document.
