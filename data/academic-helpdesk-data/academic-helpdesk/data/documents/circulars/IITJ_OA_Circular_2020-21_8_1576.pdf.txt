Subject: Rules and Regulations for conduct and Evaluation of M.Tech./M.Sc. Project joined on
0r after July 2019
In continuation to rules and regulations to evaluate the M.Sc./M. Tech. project under the
trimester system the competent authority approved the following:
1. In trimester I continuation grade will be awarded by the respective supervisor.
2. Mid term evaluation in the mid of the trimester 2nd on 18th Jan 2021 will be conducted
based on project presentation to award the letter grade to M.Sc. and M.Tech. students
for their project work which is equivalent to Semester I evaluation. Mid-
term evaluation in the middle of the trimester II may correspond to 6 credits which
would be for M. Tech Project Trimester I (3 credits) + Trimester II (3 credits) and 5 credit
which would be for M.Sc. Project Trimester I (5 credits) . Continuation grade may be
awarded at the end of trimester II to M.Sc. and M.Tech. students for their project work.
3. Final report will be submitted at the end of the trimester III and accordingly based on
the final presentation and report the remaining credit except trimester I and trimester II
of grade will be awarded to M.Sc. and M.Tech. students for their project work by conduct
and evaluation committee of M.Tech. /M.Sc. project. The Chairman DRC of respective
department will upload the grades at the New Aryabhata portal.
