CIRCULAR
Subject: Norms for engagement of Guest Faculty Member(s)
The ECS in its 25th Meeting held on 27th September 2022 has approved the
following Norms for engagement of Guest Faculty Member:
1. The Department should request the engagement of a guest faculty
member after adequate evaluation of teaching load of faculty members
in the Department;
2. The guest faculty member must teach a part of the course along with
the designated faculty member from the department;
3. In special cases, if the expertise is not available within the department or
Institute or if the guest faculty member is from industry, he/she may be
considered for teaching the complete course with a course coordinator
well within the department;
4. The Course Coordinator must coordinate all aspects of the course
including examination ' and grading with the help of guest faculty;
5. In case of engagement of Guest Faculty, the Faculty/Coordinator of the
concerned Department/Center/School/Division will be responsiblefor
the smooth conduction of examination and submission of Grades; and
6. The departments/schools must submit proper justification for
engagement of guest faculty members.
(Amardeep Sharma)
