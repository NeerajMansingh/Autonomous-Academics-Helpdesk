In order to facilitate the process of timely announcement of State of the Art Seminar,
Departments/Schools/IDRP/Center have been authorized to announcethe same.Inthis regard
checklist has been prepared for Departments/Schools/IDRP/Center before announcement of the
State of the Art Seminar by the Departments/Schools/IDRP/Centers.
Checklist for Faculty Members and Departments/Centers/Divisions/IDRP/ Schools:
1. Course work has been completed by the student
2. Comprehensive Examination has been completed with satisfactory performance in all
three papers.
3. Report of Comprehensive Examination along with question papers and answer sheets must
havebeen submitted to the Office of Academics.
4. Patent landscaping document should be submitted to the SRC for recommendation before
announcement of the soTA by the student through respective supervisor, if applicable.
5. Approval has to be obtained by the respective supervisor from DRc by submitting a report
on research.
6. WebEx link/alternate platform (for external members only) will be created by respective
HoD's/Head, IDRP's ID
7. Departments/Schools/IDRP/Centers must announce the State of the Art Seminar among
all the Ph.D.including IDRP,M.Tech.,M.Tech.-Ph.D students at least oneweek beforethe
date of SOTA keeping Office of Academics in CC.
8. Format of communication via email along with webex link in case of online seminar is
placed at AnnexureI.
9. Format of Abstract is placed at Annexure II
10. SOTA Report is placed at Annexure III needs to be submitted to the Office of Academics
along with documents related to patent landscaping (if applicable) in the domain, within
1O working days of conducting SOTA. However, a copy of SOTA report may be kept with
the supervisor and the department for future references.
This issues with the approval of Competent Authority.
