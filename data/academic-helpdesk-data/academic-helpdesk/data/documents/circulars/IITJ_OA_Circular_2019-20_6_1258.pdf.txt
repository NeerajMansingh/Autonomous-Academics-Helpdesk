Subject: Registration Kept Alive for M.Tech, M.Tech.-Ph.D. and Ph.D. Students
As per the Rules & Regulations (For Students enrolled from July 2019 onwards),
Clause no. 21.1.1
Registration Kept Alive (RKA): -
"A student admitted to an M. Tech program who has at least completed minimum credit
requirement of the first semester, can keep his studentship alive while he is pursuing any
professional activity, by opting for RKA in subsequent semesters. The candidate must indicate his
intent of RKA before the last date of late registration through DRC to Dean(academic). The
candidate has to pay appropriate registration fees for RKA status. A student can maintain RKA
status fora maximum of 6 semesters".
As per Clause no. 21.5
Maximum Duration of a Program: -
"M. Tech. (Regular and Part-time/External/Executive) - 12 registered semesters which may
include maximum 6 semesters of Registration kept Alie (RKA) after completion of the first
semester. Tuition fee post RKA zvill be same as that of sponsored candidates".
" Ph.D. and M. Tech.-Ph.D. dual degree (Regular and Part-time/External) - 14 registered
semesters which may include maximum 6 semesters of Registration kept Alive (RKA) after
completion of the tzvo semesters. Tuition fee post RKA will be same as that of sponsored candidates.
Students may request for RKA to Dean (Academics) through DRC".
In this regard, the competent authority approved the following:
1. Tuition fee as per fee structure of respective programme only be charged during
the Registration Kept Alive period.
2. Tuition fee post RKA will be same as that of sponsored candidate including
semester fee.
3. Tuition and semester fee for Executive category will be same in post RKA.
