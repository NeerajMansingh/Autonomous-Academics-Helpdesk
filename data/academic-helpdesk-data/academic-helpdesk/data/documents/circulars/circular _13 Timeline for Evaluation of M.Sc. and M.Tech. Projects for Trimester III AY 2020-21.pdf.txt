CIRCULAR
Subject: Timeline for Evaluation of M.Sc. and M.Tech. Projects for Trimester III AY 2020-21
The competent authority accorded approval for the rules and regulations for conduct and
evaluation of M.Sc./M.Tech. Project of students joined after 2019 and same was communicated
vide circular no IIT/OA/C/2020-21/8/1576 dated 27 October 2020 (Annexure-A).
2. As per the earlier approval, the final report is to be submitted at the end of the trimester II.
The dates for conduct the M.Sc. and M. Tech. Project evaluation for Trimester III is between 16-
19 May 2021.
3. The COVID 19 pandemic created a challenging situation. In order to cope with the challenges
and the extension request to be received from the students for M.Sc. and M. Tech. Project
evaluation, their evaluation is to be conducted between 17-20 June 2021.Accordingly, for such
students, a continuation grade (X) may be awarded in the grade submission for Trimester II,
which will be converted to regular grade after the evaluation is complete.
4. Further, for the M. Tech.-Ph.D. dual degree students who have not yet completed their
Comprehensive/Course work/SOTA, a continuation grade (X) may be awarded in the M. Tech.
Project Trimester II, which will be converted to the regular grade after the successful research
proposal defence as per the approved timelines for the same.
The issue with the approval of the Competent Authority.
