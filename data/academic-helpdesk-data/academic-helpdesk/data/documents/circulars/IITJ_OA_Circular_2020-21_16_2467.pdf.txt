Subject: Guideline for performance improvement of credits earned by Ph.D. students enrolled
from July 2019 is as follows:
1. While securing minimum over-all CGPA requirement in the 12 (Ph.D. in departmental
domains)/ 30(Ph,D. in IDRP domains with M.Sc. degreein
Science discipline/ direct Ph.D.) earned credits, a course repeat process may be allowed for
a maximum of 4 credits for Ph.D. in departmental domains and 8 credits for Ph.D. in IDRP
domains with M.Sc. degree in a Science discipline/direct Ph.D. The candidate can repeat
the course within the said credit limit in which he/she has obtained a pass grade
but performance is poor (secured grade is B- or lower).
2. No replacement of course(s) in which the student has secured pass grade and above is
allowed.
3. This provision will help as a grade improvement scope for candidates in Ph.D.
programmes
4. If the candidate opt to repeat a course, then the grade secured in the next attempt will
prevail.
5. The improvement policy will be applicable retrospectively from July 2019.
This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
