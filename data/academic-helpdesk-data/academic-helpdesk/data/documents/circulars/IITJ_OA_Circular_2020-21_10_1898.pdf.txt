Subject:_Rules and Regulations for conduct and Evaluation of M.Tech./M. Tech.-
Ph.D./M.Sc./M.Sc.-M.Tech.Project for the students who joined on or after July 2019
In continuation to earlier Circular No. IITJ/OA/Circular/2020-21/8/1576 dated 27
October 2020, the following procedure is to be followed to evaluate the M. Tech./M. Tech.-
Ph.D./M.Sc./M.Sc.-M.Tech. project under the trimester system:
1. The mid-term project evaluation of M.Sc. (M.Sc. project part-I of credit 5), M. Tech. and
dual degree M. Tech.-Ph.D. (M. Tech. project part-I of credit 6) has been scheduled during
18th-23rd January 2021.
2. The DRC of respective department can schedule the presentations to conduct the same
within the said timeline. For M.Sc. and M. Tech. students, the committees have already
been formed as per regulation. The SRC will evaluate the project for the dual degree M.
Tech.-Ph.D. students.
3. The presentation and the submitted report will be evaluated as per the following
parameters

The committee will award the marks after evaluation and allocate a tentative letter
grade. The DRC will ensure that the award of letter grade will be as per the relative
grading policy. The moderated letter grades by the DRC for individual student will be
entered in dully signed Form-A-23 (M.Sc. and M.Tech.)/Form-A-24. (Dual degree
M.Tech.-Ph.D) and submitted to the Office of Academics.
4. The student must attach a plagiarism report along with the project report of maximum 10
pages.
This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
