Subject: Annual Progress Monitoring Meeting (APM)
With reference to clause 26.4 Annual Progress Monitoring meeting (APM), of the
regulations for students registered from July 2019 onwards, the detailed guidelines are as
under:
1. The Annual Progress Monitoring Meeting (APM) for the thesis will be
conducted after the one year of completion of qualifier requirements;
2. Format for Annual Progress Report, will be shared separately;
3. Annual Progress Assessment Form Annexure I;
4. For Poster Presentation Departments/IDRPs/Schools/Divisions may decide the
format as per their requirement;
5. First Annual Progress Meeting (APM) of batch 2019 will be conducted by the last
working day of September 2022;
6. Proposed fixed dates for conducting annual progress evaluation are as under:
i. For students joining in 1st semester of AY: APM must be conducted by the
Last Working Date of September of every year
ii. For students joining in 2nd semester of AY: APM must be conducted by the
Last Working Date of February of every year
7. Those students who have got 6 months extension to complete qualifier
requirements for them APM can be scheduled even after a semester from the
date of completion of qualifier requirements.
This has issued with the approval of the Competent Authority.
