Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur
IITJ/OA/Circular/2019-20/3/12.06
Dated: 10th August 2020
CIRCULAR
Subject: Regarding extension of one months for M.Sc. and M.Tech. students for Project/
Thesis work
With reference to the above captioned subject it is submitted that 2oth Senate accorded approval
for the following:
"Second-year students of M.Tech. and M.Sc. can complete the thesis evaluation through the
remaining part of the project at the campus and subsequently complete the viva-voce
examination. All students who cannot complete the examination by 30 July 2020 due to genuine
reasons will be required to apply for an extension with the recommendation of the supervisor."
In view of the above, Office of Academics has received applications for extension of one month
for M.Sc. and M.Tech. students for Project/Thesis work with recommendation of Supervisor due
to current Covid-19 outbreak across India which has created extraordinary situations.
In this regard following approval have been accorded:
1. To give one month extension to Second year M.Sc. and M.Tech. students for their
Project/Thesis work till 31st August 2020.
2. Students not able to complete their Project/Thesis work till 31st August 2020 have to
register for A.Y. 2020-21 Semester I and have to pay 50% Tuition and Semester fee.
The approval has been accorded without Assistantship/Fellowship beyond permissible time
limit.
(Shakti Ranjan Patra)
Assistant Registrar (Academics)
