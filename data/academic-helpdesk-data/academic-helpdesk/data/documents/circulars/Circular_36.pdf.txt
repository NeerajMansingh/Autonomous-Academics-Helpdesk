AnnexureI
Guidelines for the new course approval through the Senate
The different Academic Units at the Institute propose new relevant elective courses
to strengthen the elective bouquet. The following guidelines to be followed while
proposing new courses:
1. The course must be discussed in the Department Faculty Board (DFB) for
another discussion with relevant faculty members of other departments, if
required;
2. The concerned Department must circulate the proposed content to all other
Departments for their input. The Departments must be given at least a time
duration of 2 weeks to provide the required inputs. The Heads must provide
the feedback of their Department to avoid overlap with existing courses and
to'modify the content;
3. After receiving the feedback. from different Departments, the concerned.
Department must send the proposed course to subject.experts for the review;
4. At least one of the subject experts must have a post Ph.D. teaching experience
of more than 10 years' preferably the course should be reviewed by the
program or by the Advisory Board of the Department;:
5. Elective Courses must have feedback from industry wherever required; and
6. Based on the reviews the contents of the course must be modified and
presented in the Academic Committee for its consideration.
Considering that the above process may require substantial time, the departments
are requested to start the process well ahead of the start.of a new semester.
