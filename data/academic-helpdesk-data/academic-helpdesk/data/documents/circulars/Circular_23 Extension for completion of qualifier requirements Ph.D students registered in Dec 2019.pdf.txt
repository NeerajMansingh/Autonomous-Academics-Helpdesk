Subject: Extension for completion of qualifier requirements Ph.D. students registered
in December 2019
With reference to clause 26.5 qualifier requirements of Ph.D. students of the
regulations for students registered from July 2019 onwards the timeline for Ph.D.
students registered in December 2019 us under:
a. Extension to complete the qualifier requirements on or before 31 July 2022 as
one time exception only; The department have to ensure to adhere to the
timeline i.e. 31 July 2022;
b. In case Ph.D. student registered in December 2019 are not able to complete the
qualifier requirements, detailed report to be submitted by the Department duly
recommended by Supervisor, SRC, DRC/IRC and Head of the
department/IDRP.
This has issued with the approval of the Competent Authority.
