CIRCULAR
Subject: Submission of Grades for Research Proposal in respect of Ph.D. students registered
from July 2019 onwards
In continuation to the circular No. ITJ/OA/Circular/2020-21/12/2116 dated 24 February 2021
regarding Format for submission of Research Proposal for Ph.D. students registered from July
2019 onwards, and as per clause 26.5.3 of the regulations, Research Proposal (report +
presentation) will be evaluated by SRC after successful completion of SOTA. The research
proposal must include representative research work equivalent to research work worth 16 credits.
SRC will allocate a letter grade based on the performance of the candidate.
In addition to the above regulations the following may be noted;
1. It will be the responsibility of the supervisor/SRC to ensure that the students are
working for equivalent hours in the credited research proposal course for the
trimester/semester.
2. The upload of the grade (continuation/unsatisfactory) in the particular research
proposal course in a trimester/semester will be done in consultation with the
supervisor/SRC.
3. After successful Research Proposal defense, SRC will allocate a letter grade based on the
performance of the candidate in consultation with supervisor.
This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
