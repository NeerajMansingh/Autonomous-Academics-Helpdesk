Subject: Extension for completion of qualifier requirements Ph.D. students registered in July
2019.
With reference to the clause 26.5 qualifier requirement of Ph.D. student of the regulations
for students registered from July 2019 onwards the timeline for Ph.D. students registered in July
2019 is under:
a.
 Extension to complete the qualifier requirements on or before 31 December 2021 as one-
time exception only; The department have to ensure, to adhere to the timeline i.e. 31
December 2021;
b. In case Ph.D. student registered in July 2019 are not able to complete the qualifier
requirements, detailed report to be submitted by the Department duly recommended
by Supervisor, SRC, DRC/IRC and Head of department/IDRP.
This has issued with the approval of the Competent Authority.
