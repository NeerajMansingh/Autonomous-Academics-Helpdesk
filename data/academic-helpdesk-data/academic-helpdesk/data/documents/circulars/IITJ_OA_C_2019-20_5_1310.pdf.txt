Subject: Regarding Publication requirement for Submission of Ph.D. Thesis
With reference to the above cited subject, the Competent Authority has approved the
following:
The submission of Ph.D. Thesis vould require at least trvo publications in Scopus indexed journals
and/or A+ conferences for Engineering and Science Departments. Requirements for the Department of
Humanities and School of Management & Entrepreneurship vill be specified separately.
The above will be applicable to all existing Ph.D. students who are yet to submit their Ph.D.
thesis.
